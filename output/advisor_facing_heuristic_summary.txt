Advisor-facing heuristic-stage summary

Benchmark setup
- Persistent-fleet AMoD-style simulation with request queue and persistent vehicle states.
- Frozen operating point: fleet_size=300, dispatch_interval=15s, request_scale=0.005, candidate caps=15/15.
- Controlled reproducible comparison with shared scenario settings and controlled randomness.

Heuristic-stage result
- Policy A (no rebalancing) remains a strong baseline.
- Naive Policy B and naive Policy C did not improve over A at this operating point.
- A minimal gated demand-share variant (C_gated fix1) performed best in this benchmark.

Final benchmark-specific ranking
- C_gated_fix1 > A > C > B (by served requests; waiting-end consistent).

Cautious interpretation
- This result is benchmark-specific and operating-point-specific.
- We do not claim universal superiority of one heuristic across all AMoD settings.
- The evidence supports that at this operating point, lightly gated demand-share rebalancing reduced empty-move churn enough to outperform the naive alternatives.

Literature-aligned framing
- Persistent fleet dynamics before rebalancing evaluation.
- Reproducible baseline-first comparison before advanced methods.
- Naive rebalancing can be counterproductive in a given benchmark, while targeted and throttled repositioning can perform better.
