diagnosis

- C reduces this burden substantially and is closer to A, but still introduces extra empty movement and mild mistiming compared with A's pure local assignment behavior.
- At this load level, demand pressure is moderate enough that frequent proactive repositioning can be counterproductive.

interpretation
- most likely reason for ranking A > C > B: excessive and mistimed empty repositioning, strongest in B, moderate in C.
- both B and C stay safe (0 teleports, 0 collisions), so this is an efficiency issue rather than a safety failure.
